About

Loom stacks 2D slices into a single 3D scalar field, then renders it by casting a ray through every pixel — so what you see is the whole reconstructed volume, not a surface mesh fitted to it. Geometry comes from the file headers: in-plane pixel spacing and slice position, so proportions stay physically correct.

Research and engineering tool. Not a medical device; not for diagnostic use.

Everything runs locally in this tab. No image data leaves the machine.
